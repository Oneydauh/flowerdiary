FlowerDiary

FlowerDiary is a manifestation journal and vision board app. Organize wishes and goals, record gratitude, feelings and progress, explore writing, releasing and audio practices, and revisit your journey in your journal.

01 · Who it is for

  • People new to manifestation who want to start with a gratitude entry or one goal.
  • People who want to keep their vision board, journal and everyday practices together.
  • People with an established practice who want to save scripts, create SUB audio and revisit different chapters of life.

02 · What you can do

Vision board & goals

Organize goals with words and pictures, record your new identity and goal status, and open a goal’s menu to practice affirmations.

How to use it

Journal whenever you like

Record gratitude, feelings, manifestation signs and limiting beliefs, with optional photos. For feelings, selecting mood tags is enough.

How to use it

Scripting & 369 writing

Scripts organize writing by scenes from your day. The 369 practice has three morning, six noon and nine evening repetitions. Save your work to add it to your journal.

How to use it

Beliefs & releasing

Record recurring thoughts, then explore releasing through questions on cards or your own guided audio.

How to use it

Guided meditation

Mandarin body and breath awareness practices for adults, currently available in 3-, 5-, 12- and 19-minute options. Try 3 or 5 minutes first. Read the notice before starting; pause or stop whenever you like. These practices do not provide diagnosis or treatment.

How to use it

Create & listen to SUB

Record your voice or convert text to speech, then mix it with background sound. Save your creations in the audio library, with repeat playback and a sleep timer.

How to use it

Book of Answers

Ask a question and draw a random response from the built-in collection. Save questions and answers you like as letters. A free usage allowance is included; see the in-app purchase screen for additional benefits.

How to use it

Review, statistics & sharing

Find entries in Today, Calendar and Garden. Usage statistics summarize entries across chapters, and journal pages can be shared as images.

05 · Free version or subscription?

The app is free to download. Start with the free version and subscribe if you need more.

PlanIncluded
Free versionUp to 3 goals in the current chapter, 1 saved audio item, 1 archived chapter and 1 release guidance recording. The Book of Answers includes a free usage allowance. Ordinary journal entries and release practices have no usage quota.
SubscriptionRemoves these quantity limits, adds Book of Answers benefits, and unlocks all themes and fonts and premium sharing styles. See the in-app purchase screen for current benefits.

Purchase & restore

Subscriptions are purchased through Apple, with monthly, quarterly and annual auto-renewing plans. Prices and offers are shown in the app before you confirm a purchase.

Profile → Upgrade subscription (Subscription management when subscribed). Restore purchases on the purchase page.

Cancel auto-renewal

To cancel: device Settings → your name → Subscriptions → FlowerDiary → Cancel Subscription. Apple’s official instructions
