Flower Diary User Agreement

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Service Description

Flower Diary (the “App”) is a personal-growth journal and practice tool offering journals, goals, exercises, summaries, custom audio creation, and private link or local-file bookmarks.

2. Storage and iCloud Sync

a. Content you create or explicitly import is stored locally. When iCloud is available, journals, goals, practices, bookmark folders, links and imported bookmark files, images, and completed custom audio or video are synchronized through Apple CloudKit to the private database associated with your Apple ID so they can be restored and synchronized across your devices. b. CloudKit and iCloud are provided by Apple and governed by Apple’s terms. Timing and completion depend on network access, iCloud status, system limits, and available quota. c. Uninstalling the App removes only the local copy on that device and does not automatically delete data retained in iCloud. Deletions made inside the App are synchronized through CloudKit.

3. Subscriptions and Purchases

Trials, subscriptions, renewals, cancellations, and refunds are handled by Apple App Store and StoreKit. Actual pricing and available offers are shown on the purchase screen. You can manage subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.

The App is for personal journaling and growth support only. It does not provide medical, psychological, legal, or financial advice and does not guarantee any particular result from manifestation, meditation, affirmations, or similar practices. If you are experiencing a mental-health issue or serious distress, seek help from a qualified professional.

Hz values describe sound frequency, not a level of therapeutic effectiveness. The App does not claim that particular frequencies treat disease, repair DNA, or guarantee manifestation results. Start at a low device volume and adjust to your listening comfort; stop playback if you experience discomfort.

Meditation content is intended for adults aged 18 or over. Read the before-practice notice before first use and after material notice updates, and choose whether to begin. Try 3 or 5 minutes first. You may keep your eyes open, breathe naturally, adjust your posture, pause or stop whenever you wish. Discomfort does not prove benefit and need not be endured. Seek qualified professional help for symptoms that remain intense, persistent or disruptive after stopping. You are responsible for content you create, import, bookmark, or share. Saving a third-party link does not grant the App or you rights to that content. Follow the source platform’s rules, do not use the App unlawfully or infringe others’ rights, and ensure you have the right to use imported documents, images, audio, or video.

6. Built-in Background Sounds

The built-in background sounds were synthesized programmatically by the developer for this App. Rain, ocean, and singing-bowl sounds are synthetic soundscapes; fixed-frequency tones are synthesized waveforms. They use no third-party recordings, music excerpts, or audio samples. You may use these built-in sounds within the App to preview, create, save, and export your mixes. Text-to-speech uses Apple system voices, which are separate from the developer-created background sounds. Providing built-in sounds does not grant you copyright or other rights to content you import yourself. Ensure you have the necessary permissions for text, recordings, music, images, and videos when importing, mixing, exporting, or sharing them. The absence of DRM does not mean content is free to use.
