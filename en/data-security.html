Flower Diary Data Security Statement

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Storage Architecture

Flower Diary uses a “local device copy + private user iCloud” architecture. Local data is managed by Core Data and the iOS app sandbox. Cross-device data, including bookmark folders, links, and imported bookmark files, is synchronized through the user’s private Apple CloudKit database, with imported files, images, and completed custom audio or video stored as external binary assets.

2. Technical Measures

a. The App uses the iOS app sandbox, Core Data, and CloudKit and relies on Apple’s protections for data in transit and at rest. b. Private journal content is never written to the CloudKit public database used by the optional referral feature. c. The App does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

d. Built-in background sounds are stored in the App bundle. Working files for recording, speech synthesis, and mixing are processed in the App sandbox and are not uploaded to developer servers or third-party audio-generation services. Saved results and affirmation text follow the private iCloud synchronization rules above and may also be included in backups you explicitly export.

3. Data Lifecycle

a. Creation and editing: Files are safely staged before the database transaction is committed. If saving fails, database changes are reverted and newly staged files are removed. Links received by the Share extension are staged in the developer’s App Group and removed only after the Core Data save succeeds. b. Sync: When iCloud is available, CloudKit synchronizes data among devices using the same Apple ID. Large files may take longer. c. Deletion: In-app deletion commits the database deletion before local files are removed, and the deletion then synchronizes to iCloud. d. Uninstallation: Only the local device copy is removed; an iCloud copy may remain.

4. Risk Assessment

a. Sync depends on Apple ID status, network access, iCloud availability, system scheduling, and remaining quota and cannot be guaranteed to finish immediately. b. Deleting the App, disabling iCloud, or losing a device before an upload completes may still cause unsynchronized content to be lost. Keep a separate copy of important content.

5. Contact

If you have questions about data security, contact flowerdiary.app@gmail.com.

Meditation playback and notice-reading records do not upload automatically to the developer. Feedback you choose to post on the App Store or Xiaohongshu is handled by that platform, as explained in the privacy policy; it is separate from your private iCloud journal. Do not submit unnecessary health information.
